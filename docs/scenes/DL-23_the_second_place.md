## 4. The puzzle

**Central inference:** this dock wants a connection, not a weight. Someone disconnected it on purpose.

**Elements (3):** Rowan's chair (carry, place); the coupling (Use to plug); the second keyed dock (with its lamp).

**Plausible wrong assumptions, and how the world corrects each:**
- **A weight on the dock** (DL-21: a stool on its place contact, or sitting in the second place). He stands on the
  dark dock: the plate doesn't give, nothing clicks, the lamp stays dark. He keys the unfolded chair in with no
  coupling: the foot drops home with a deep, promising clunk, and still the lamp stays dark. Both the dock's socket
  and the chair foot's socket are empty brass collars in the lamp's pool. The coupling's free plug hangs from its
  coil a hand's width away, swaying in the draught from the door. At her
  dock, the same three parts are joined and lit. Sitting in the chair changes nothing either.
- **Moving Mara's chair across** (DL-20's furniture-shifting). He grabs it and pulls. The coupling from its foot draws
  taut, the taped plug holds, and the chair rocks back onto its dock. It never moves more than 0.1.
- **Half a connection.** He plugs the coupling's dock end with no chair there. The other plug lies on the floor, and
  the lamp stays dark: a lead to nothing.

**Solution:**
1. Take Rowan's chair off its peg and set it at the second dock. It unfolds, and its brass foot keys in.
2. Plug the coupling between the dock and the chair foot. The second lamp lights.
3. The two-note chime sounds for the first time in twenty years. The table rises.
4. The arm comes back along its rail from the kitchen, down through the hatch, and sets bowl 1 (her bowl, already
   there: it lifts it and sets it square on its ring), then sets Rowan's stapled bowl at the empty place. The table
   holds.
5. Climb Mara's chair, the table (stepping between the bowls) and the balcony.

He can plug the dock end before the chair is there; the lamp lights when the last plug goes in.

**Reset:** none needed. Unplugging (Use at the connected coupling) puts out lamp 2 and stows the table in 2 s, the
bowls riding down. Re-plugging chimes and raises it at once; the arm's serve is latched, and doesn't repeat.

**Checklist (principles 1–14).** Meets all. Rule 2: one new thing, the coupling, on DL-21's routine. Rule 4: DL-21's
weight lesson, corrected by a visible missing plug. Rule 9: re-routing a household's power is a courier's work, and
it delivers a bowl. Rule 12: one authored shot, which shows nothing the side view doesn't.

## 5. New models

- **The upper flat's dining room:** the front door (ajar, on its latch); plaster walls, the end wall under the balcony;
  the balcony with its slab, front rail from 430.6 on, and two empty ladder hooks on the lip, worn bright.
- **By the door:** two coat hooks, her coat on one (neat darns, pipe tape on a cuff); the dresser and the rota card.
- **The docks:** two keyed docks, each a floor plate with a slot shaped to a chair's brass foot, and a short post at its
  back edge with a socket (brass collar) and a caged dock lamp.
- **Mara's chair:** steel frame, brass foot, her later work clothes over the back (overalls patched at the knees, a
  shirt). Its coupling runs foot to socket, the plug bound with black tape and a twist of wire.
- **Rowan's chair:** a wooden folding chair, painted with leaves and wandering roots, his root glyph on the back rail,
  a patched cushion tied on, and a brass foot fitted later, the paint chipped round it. Its top rail is worn bright.
  Behind it on the wall, the wall peg and the chair's unfaded outline.
- **The coupling:** a black braided lead (1.2) with a keyed brass plug at each end, coiled in three loops on a hook,
  tied with a strip of the lower flat's apron print.
- **The table:** the evening routine's table on its lifting column. Two pale rings in its varnish, one at each place.
  Her plain bowl and her cup at her end.
- **The two-note chime:** two tuned pipe offcuts on a bracket on the back wall at 1.7, each with a striker. Both are
  dull with dust; the dust puffs off when they are struck.
- **The serving arm** (seen through the hatch): its forearm and padded gripper, and its work lamp behind. Rowan's
  stapled bowl waits on the rack beyond the hatch, at the serving rail's end.
- **The lamp on a long flex**, looped along the ceiling from a socket by the door to hang over the table.

## 10. Acceptance checks

- A first-time player who stands on the dark dock sees and hears nothing change, with the free plug swaying in frame
  within 1 m of him.
- Keying the chair in with no coupling: the clunk within 0.5 s of setting it down, and the lamp still dark 2 s later.
  At 720p the empty socket collars are at least 10 px, and her joined lead reads as a line in the same frame.
- Pulling Mara's chair: the lead draws taut within 0.5 s and the chair rocks back; it never moves more than 0.1.
- From the last plug to bowl 2 landing is 14.4 s; to the peak 29.0 s; control returns at 37.5 s. The moment never
  replays after a reload, an unplug or a re-plug.
- With the table held, her chair → table → lip climbs in under 6 s. With the table stowed, no route reaches the lip.
- No text on screen says Rowan died; every prop carries only initials, glyphs, ticks and years.
- Mechanical floor (simulated player, no hesitation): about 1:05, including the 37.5 s moment. First-play target:
  median 3:00, range 2:15–4:00.
