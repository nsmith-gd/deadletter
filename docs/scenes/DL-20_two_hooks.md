# DL-20 Two Hooks: production plan

**Status:** design for director review, 26 October 2026 (task C-01, #6). Nothing here is built.
**Source:** `docs/chapter2_plan.md` §4 DL-20 and §6; `docs/full_game_plan.md` §7 DL-20.
**Sequence:** DL-19 → DL-20 → DL-21, with no cut on either side.
**Duration target:** 2.0 minutes of first play (chapter plan §3). The puzzle itself stays under one minute.

## 1. What the scene must do

| Requirement | How the scene meets it |
|---|---|
| A quiet relationship reveal before any loss | The lower flat's front room: two hooks with two coats, two pairs of boots, two sketches pinned into one picture, two quilts, two place-lamps. Preferences and humour, nothing missing. |
| A short application, not a new trick (§7: "not a stretched search") | DL-18's thought again, now for two: the **shelf** stands where the **second bed section** unfolds. Clear the space, unfold the section, climb it. No designed wrong assumption. |
| Belongings carry the proof, not the bed | The bed only says "two" with its second quilt. The coats, the bowl, the sketches and the score make the two people specific. DL-23's single coat on two hooks pays this off. |
| Safe motion | Nothing can hurt D-17. The section stops and waits for him; the shelf rolls on its own worn ruts. |
| One moment of play that no puzzle needs | **The marble run** (chapter plan §6): a jar on the doorway sill, two lanes down the wall, a bell, and Rowan's chalked score: the pipes win every race, and never the score. |
| Seed the next scene | Through the doorway, every 14 s, one pipe note: DL-21's evening routine asking for its second place. |

## 4. New models

- **The front room:** the doorway from the terrace, two hooks on a board, two coats, two pairs of boots; the window
  onto the shaft, its sill lined with empty pots, each labelled with Rowan's root glyph.
- **The sketches:** two sheets pinned edge to edge, her pipes and his roots, one tree between them.
- **The fold bed:** DL-18's model and her same patched quilt, here in the lower flat's bed recess.
- **The second bed section:** a hand-made leaf in a paler, different timber, joined with visible dovetails. It hangs
  on two hinges made from pipe clamps (her hand) and is painted with a root along its rail (his). It has folding legs,
  a worn leather strap and a second quilt of bright mismatched patches, from the same cloth as his coat's patches.
- **The shelf:** a dresser on castors, 0.8 wide, 1.1 tall, with a fiddle rail. A worn steel grip on each side. On it:
  Rowan's chipped bowl, a jar of seed, a tin of pins, and her pliers.
- **The alcove:** a niche in the back wall, the wallpaper inside unfaded where the shelf has always stood at night.
- **The place-lamps:** two small wall lamps over the pillows, one (over hers) with no bulb.
- **The marble run:** her lane in copper pipe offcuts with neat soldered bends; his in carved wooden roots that
  wander. A brass bell cup where they meet. The jar of marbles on the sill. The chalked score above the lintel.

## 9. The puzzle

**A short application by design.** The full game plan's §5 allows quiet interactions without a trick, and this is
one: it follows a principal puzzle (DL-19) and comes before a developing one (DL-21). It must stay under
a minute of play, because it repeats DL-18's thought (chapter plan §8).

- **Central inference:** none new. DL-18's "two things share one space; clear one to have the other", applied with
  confidence. The worn ruts say where the shelf lives, and the strap behind it says what it is in the way of.
- **Plausible wrong assumptions:** none designed. Two honest failures:
  - **Climb the shelf.** It already stands under the doorway and looks like a step. D-17 reaches up the shelf's face,
    gets no grip, and drops back within 1 s: 1.1 is out of his reach. The fiddle rail and the bowl on top say "not
    a step" before he tries.
  - **Push it the other way.** It moves 0.15 and knocks against the folded section's legs. The ruts run the other way.
- **Elements (2):** the shelf (grab: push or pull along its ruts); the second bed section (Use at its strap).
- **Solution:**
  1. Grab the shelf and pull it along its ruts. Past the curve it rolls home into its alcove by itself.
  2. Use the strap. The section comes down and D-17 backs off with it.
  3. Climb the section (0.45) and the doorway sill (0.95).
- **Cheap reset:** everything moves on input. The shelf can be pulled back out of its alcove, and the section folds
  up again on Use. Wherever the shelf stops short of its alcove, it is plainly in the way of either the section or
  D-17 himself, and one more pull fixes it.
- **If the section meets the shelf:** it cannot happen from the start state, because the strap is behind the shelf. If
  the player pulls the shelf out of its alcove again and then lowers the section, the section rests on the shelf's
  rail at about 40°, as DL-18's bench rests on the mattress. Climbing it slides him off. Pulling the shelf out from
  under it lets it settle flat.

## 10. Acceptance checks

- **Mechanical floor:** about 20 s from the front door to the sill, with no hesitation. **First play:** median 2.0
  minutes for the scene, and the puzzle (entering the bedroom framing to the sill) under 60 s for at least 80% of
  first-time players.
- On entering the bedroom framing, the shelf, the strap above it, the ruts, the alcove and the lit jar on the sill are
  all in frame and readable at 720p on a handheld screen. D-17 is never more than one pull from the answer.
- A first-time player who tries to climb the shelf sees D-17 reach and drop back within 1 s, and is in control again
  at once.
- A player who grabs the shelf and pulls left sees it pass the curve and roll home within 4 s of grabbing.
- A player who Uses the strap sees the section land within 1.5 s; D-17 is never under it and never pushed.
- From the section, the sill mantle succeeds first time from anywhere under the doorway (386.0 to 386.8).
- Use at the jar facing left rings the bell in 2.6 s (copper lane); facing right, in 4.4 s (root lane). The copper
  marble is always first. Any stick input cancels `Follow` within 0.2 s; nothing else changes and no hint ever points
  at the run.
- `marbles` plays on the first marble only, and never after a checkpoint reload.
