# D-17

A procedural Blender build of D-17 from the front and side turnaround concept, with a
rig and a looping walk cycle. Everything is generated by `build_d17.py`, so the
model and animation can be rebuilt and tuned from source.

## Contents

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `build_d17.py` | Builds geometry, materials, rig, walk cycle, lights and cameras, then renders |
| `assemble.py` | Turns rendered frames into a looping GIF/MP4 or a sprite sheet |
| `d17_walk.blend` | Saved scene for the tracking walk shot (open in Blender 5.2+) |
| `renders/` | Turnaround and walk loops, plus a side-view sprite sheet |

## Construction, matched to the concept

- **Head:** a painted slab shell 9 mm thick.
  - It is open underneath, and its bottom edge climbs toward the back.
  - The rear-bottom is cut away around a sensor dial, and a camera module sticks out the back.
  - Countersunk eye holes show the dark sensors behind them. The tall oval ear ports sit near the front.
  - On top: two tipped flaps on hinges, a rubber knob, a coil spring and the antenna socket.
- **Neck:** a gimbal inside a dark cavity, lit by a warm lamp under the front lip.
- **Body:**
  - A dented, wide, shallow pod with a rolled and riveted lip, and warm indicator slits.
  - A backpack of drum motors (seen end-on from the side) with straps, a translucent canister and a cream block.
  - Cables hang in loops underneath.
- **Shoulders:** a cloth-wrapped motor on the right; on the left, a bracket, a motor and a hanging pale plate.
- **Claw arms:**
  - A dark strut with a piston.
  - A forearm made of two cream sleeves split by a dark collar.
  - A palm with a thumb and two broad blades. The left claw rests open and the right rests closed.
- **Legs:** twin thigh struts with a piston, a chunky knee actuator, a weathered shin, and a bird-like foot with cream toes splayed sideways.

## Rig

- `root`: ground motion. Keyed forward in the walking shot so planted feet don't slide.
- `body`, `head`: bob, sway and lagged head motion.
- `antenna_1..3`: skinned chain with follow-through.
- `ik_foot.L/R`, `ik_hand.L/R`: IK targets for the legs and claw arms. The pole
  targets (`pole_knee`, `pole_elbow`) are auto-calibrated at build time.
- `finger_a/b.L/R`: claw open and close. Each claw also opens during its swing.

Rigid parts are parented to bones (no skin weights), except the antenna. This makes
the rig suitable for FBX export to Unity, or for rendering 2D sprites.

## Walk cycle

- 36 frames (1.5 s at 24 fps), a four-beat gait: left leg, left claw, right leg, right claw.
- Planted limbs travel 0.11 m over 70% of the cycle.
- `SPEED` is derived from those values, so the root and the feet always agree.

## Rebuild

```sh
pip install bpy==5.2.2 pillow imageio-ffmpeg      # Python 3.13
python build_d17.py --shot turnaround --out out/turnaround
python build_d17.py --shot hero       --out out/hero --blend d17_walk.blend
python build_d17.py --shot sprites    --out out/sprites
python assemble.py video out/turnaround renders/d17_turnaround
python assemble.py video out/hero renders/d17_walk --loops 1
python assemble.py sheet out/sprites renders/d17_walk_sprites.png
```

Add `--shot closeup --still N --samples 16` for a quick single-frame check.
